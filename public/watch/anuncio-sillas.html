New chair update

This announcement shows the upgraded barber chairs at El Caché 10. Better seating improves comfort for longer services such as detailed fades, beard work, hot towel shaves and blow dry sessions.

Barbers interested in chair rental can call the shop directly to ask about availability, schedule, requirements and current terms.

Why the chair upgrade matters

Comfortable, stable chairs help both the client and the barber. They make longer services easier, support cleaner angles for fades and beard work, and create a more professional setup for anyone renting a station.

For clients, this means a better experience during detailed services such as skin fades, hot towel shaves, blow dry styling and kids cuts. For barbers, it means a ready shop environment with walk-in traffic and local visibility.

If you are a licensed or experienced barber looking for a chair in the Bronx, call El Caché 10 before visiting. The team can explain schedule expectations, current availability and what type of work fits the shop.
